Privacy Policy

Blue Rock QuickBooks Desktop Integration

Last Updated: July 10, 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy describes how the Blue Rock QuickBooks integration application ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when connecting to QuickBooks Online via the Intuit API.

This App is a private, internal tool used solely by Blue Rock for managing its own QuickBooks data. It is not distributed publicly or made available to third parties.

2. Information We Access

The App accesses QuickBooks Online financial data belonging to Blue Rock, including but not limited to:

  • Customer and vendor records
  • Invoices and payment information
  • Account balances and transaction data
  • Other financial records as needed for business operations

3. How Information Is Used

All data accessed through the QuickBooks API is used exclusively for Blue Rock's internal business operations. Data is never sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Data accessed by the App is stored locally on a secured device at Blue Rock's premises.
  • The App uses OAuth 2.0 authentication as required by Intuit.
  • Client credentials (Client ID and Client Secret) are stored securely and are not hardcoded in the application.
  • Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely and handled according to Intuit's security guidelines.

5. Data Retention

Data retrieved from QuickBooks Online is retained only as long as necessary for Blue Rock's business purposes. When data is no longer needed, it is deleted from local storage.

6. Third Party Sharing

This App does not share, sell, rent, or trade any QuickBooks data with third parties. All data remains within Blue Rock's control.

7. Compliance

This App complies with Intuit's Developer Terms of Service, Intuit's platform requirements for data handling and security, and applicable Australian privacy laws.
